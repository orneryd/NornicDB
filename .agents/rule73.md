# Rule 73 — change the causal approach

Load this file when Direct or Stage 2 enters Rule 73. The route module owns the trigger and correction unit: Direct uses its complete bounded target; Stage 2 uses the affected implementation family.

Rule 73 ends ordinary per-finding and per-consumer correction for that unit. Re-establish its complete current boundary and accepted correction set, identify the shared causal owner, architecture, mechanism, or method that let the failures survive, and correct that whole causal unit through every affected consumer as one integrated implementation.

The next candidate is ready for review only when the changed causal approach is implemented across the complete unit and its unit-wide proof passes under `AGENTS.md` section 9, **Verification and completion**, including its response to recurring failures. Intermediate implementation and test failures are feedback inside the same Rule 73 work; they are not ready candidates, correction attempts, or reasons to launch review.

When a Rule 73 candidate fails its required review or live proof, Rule 73 remains active. Reassess the causal approach shown to have failed and materially change it before producing a successor. Do not return to leaf correction or launch another review until the complete integrated successor and its proof are ready.

Rule 73 has no fixed technical recipe. Follow the evidence and remain inside the applicable correction unit. It finishes only when the integrated current unit passes its required review and proof.
